Run: `python main.py` from this directory. Keyboard: **P** play/restart, **S** stop, **N** next, **B** previous, **Q** quit. Shows current track, index, state and playback progress. Playback advances automatically and wraps around the playlist. Three original short WAV demo tones are provided in `music/sample_tracks/`; add MP3, WAV or OGG files anywhere inside `music/` to extend the playlist. An available audio output device is required.
